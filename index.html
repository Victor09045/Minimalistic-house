Minimalist House

A walkable 3D house with a detailed bathroom and objects you can use.

The 3D view could not start in this browser. Try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari with hardware acceleration turned on.

W A S D or arrow keys to move Drag to look around Shift to walk faster E uses the object under the dot Mouse wheel zooms in dollhouse view

Click to use: the three sliding doors, bedside lamps, floor lamp and vanity light, both window blinds, the shower head or valve, the toilet lid, the laptop on the desk and the bed (click it to lie down and look at the ceiling). Doors start closed. The clock on the wall shows your real time.
